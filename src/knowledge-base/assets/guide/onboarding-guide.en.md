## Where to put notes
- **Quick thoughts**: put them in {inbox}; no need to categorize them first.
- **Articles, references and reading notes**: use {raw} or any other bound source folder.
- **Journal**: use the calendar or Journal on Home. Entries go to {journal}.

When unsure, use {inbox}.

## How to ask
Ask directly in the conversation on the right to search notes across your vault:
- What ideas have I written about pricing?
- Summarize the books I read this month.

Use `/ask` to search your bound sources, knowledge and projects with citations. It excludes the Inbox. The current locations are {raw}, {wiki} and {projects}.

## How to maintain Knowledge
New notes are searchable automatically. To turn sources into lasting knowledge, enter `/maintain` in the conversation. It uses model credits and saves the results in {wiki}.
